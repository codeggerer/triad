How to Play TRIAD

Each of the 81 cards has 4 attributes with 3 values each: Count (1, 2, 3), Color (Coral, Teal, Amber), Shape (Hex, Ellipse, Wave), and Fill (Open, Striped, Solid).

The Golden Rule: Three cards form a valid Triad if, for each individual attribute, all three cards are either all the same or all different.
✓ Valid Triad (all attributes different)

Count: 1, 2, 3 (all different) · Color: Coral, Teal, Amber (all different) · Shape & Fill: also all different.

✗ Not a Triad (Color breaks the rule)

Two cards are Coral and one is Amber — neither all the same nor all different.

  • Minimum 9 Cards: Classic mode always keeps at least 9 cards on the table. Finding a Triad on 9 cards refills 3 new random cards from the deck until the draw pile is empty.
  • No Triad!: If no valid Triad exists on the table, tap No Triad! to earn bonus points and deal 3 more random cards — until every card has been played and no Triad remains.
  • Smart Speed Scoring: Finding Triads faster earns more points, and the computer factors in how many Triads were available: spotting the sole Triad pays far more than picking quickly on a board with 8 or 10 Triads. Completed runs save your Best Time and Highscore.
